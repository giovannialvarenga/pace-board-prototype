## Part 2: Pre-Session Brief

*Send 24 hours before the scheduled session. Keep it as a clean document — PDF or plain text.*

### PACE Board — Pre-Session Brief

**Your session is confirmed. Here's what you need to know before we start.**

---

#### What PACE Board is

PACE Board is a structured AI-assisted consulting session. It interviews you about a real business challenge, identifies the assumptions you're treating as facts, maps your operational reality in five layers, and produces a complete direction document. It is not a planning template. It is not a business school exercise. It produces work you can act on this week.

The methodology behind it has been used inside organizations ranging from 12 to 200+ people. The agents that run it have been built and tested internally. This session is the first time we're running it on someone else's business.

---

#### What to prepare

**Bring one challenge.** It should be something real — an idea you're trying to validate, a decision you keep deferring, a business move you want to accelerate. The more specific, the better. "I want to grow the company" is not a challenge. "I'm deciding whether to hire a sales lead or invest in outbound infrastructure" is.

**Protect the time.** The session runs 40 to 50 minutes. No multitasking. This is not a call where you can half-attend.

**Come ready to be challenged.** The session will surface assumptions you haven't questioned. That's the point. If everything you believe about your challenge is confirmed, we've done something wrong.

---

#### Session flow — what to expect

| Step | What happens | Approx. time |
|---|---|---|
| **01 — Discovery** | The AI consultant interviews you. One question at a time. Listens for what's underneath the stated problem. | ~10 min |
| **02 — Upstream** | Before any plan, we surface your false assumptions, systemic leverage, and real bottleneck. | ~8 min |
| **03 — Map** | A 5-layer World Model of your reality + a Worldview of how you see market, customer, and competition. | ~10 min |
| **04 — Movement Plan** | A decision document for your focus initiative — calibrated to your context, not a template. | ~10 min |
| **05 — Prompts** | 3 AI-ready prompts you can use immediately after the session. | ~5 min |

---

#### What you receive and when

Within 24 hours of the session, you'll receive five artifacts:

1. **Upstream Opinion** — your top 3 false assumptions + real bottleneck with falsification tests
2. **World Model** — 5-layer map of your operational reality
3. **Worldview** — 1–2 page narrative on market, customer, competition, and decision principles
4. **Movement Plan** — complete direction document for your focus initiative
5. **Trial Prompts** — 3 prompts ready to paste into Claude or ChatGPT

---

#### One note before we start

This is not a demo. We are not showing you a product feature.

Come with a real problem. The session only works if the input is real. We'll see you then.

— Giovanni
